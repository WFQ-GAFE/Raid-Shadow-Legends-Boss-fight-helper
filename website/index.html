01 / CAPABILITIES

Everything in view. Decisions in your hands.

Prepare a strategy, understand the live encounter, and review what your rules actually do.

01✦

Chimera trials & simulation

Track forms, trial chains, effects, and damage. Replay a saved opening with edited rules, or forecast a new battle from its start to check trial and damage goals.

Simulation: experimental
02◇

Hydra devour-order forecast

Set a condition such as “do not mark this champion in the first N marks.” An opening forecast checks the expected order and, once verified against live turns, can inform a guarded regroup.

Experimental
03▦

Team setup preview

See the selected champions’ stats, sets, blessings, relics, skills, and masteries. Include a team snapshot when sharing a strategy.

04↳

Rules that match your plan

Use ordered rules, conditions, target priorities, and separate strategy profiles for Chimera and Hydra. Follow each run in its own log.

Experimental forecasts need an opening capture and validation against live turns. Unvalidated results are not used to regroup, and predictions are not guaranteed.

02 / GET STARTED

From download to your first strategy.

Runs locally on Windows with the RAID: Shadow Legends game client.

  1. 01

    Get the Windows app

    Open the latest GitHub Release and download the RSL-Boss-helper executable.

  2. 02

    Choose your battle

    Start the app, select your running RAID account, and choose the Chimera or Hydra workspace. Select your team on the game’s preparation screen.

  3. 03

    Set goals and rules

    Save a strategy profile, set targets and priorities, then follow the battle. For opening simulations or forecasts, enable the feature and take over from the start.

03 / THE PROJECT

Built in the open.

RSL-Boss-helper is a local, open-source Windows project for players who want more control over their Boss strategy. Explore the code, read the documentation, or join the community.

Independent community project. Not affiliated with RAID: Shadow Legends or Plarium. Automated play may violate game rules and carry account risk.
