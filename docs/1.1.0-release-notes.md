# RSL-Boss-helper 1.1.0

## 中文

- **奇美拉策略模拟：** 用已保存的战斗开局检查当前规则能否完成必要试炼、达到伤害目标，并找出可能卡住的英雄和规则。从开局接管一场奇美拉战斗后，在「策略模拟」选择该场数据和模拟场数，点击「模拟当前规则」查看报告。
- **奇美拉开局整场模拟：** 开战后预估本场目标能否完成；预测与已发生的实战回合一致且目标预计失败时，按设定的次数上限免费重整。在「战斗目标」设置必要试炼或最低伤害，开启「开局模拟整场战斗」，然后从开局接管。
- **六头蛇开局吞噬顺序推演：** 提前检查吞噬标记顺序是否符合已设的重整条件，也可同时检查预计最终伤害。在六头蛇「战斗目标 → 吞噬顺序重整」设置条件，例如「前 N 次不能标记指定英雄」，勾选「开局离线推演吞噬顺序（实验）」，再从开局接管战斗。
- **队伍配置预览与分享：** 查看所选英雄的属性、套装、祝福、圣物、技能和专精，并在导出策略时附上队伍配置。在 Boss 准备界面选好队伍后，打开「当前队伍 → 队伍配置」；导入带有配置的策略后，打开「作者的队伍」查看。

## English

- **Chimera strategy simulation:** Check whether the current rules complete required trials and meet the damage goal, and identify champions or rules that may stall. Take over a Chimera battle from its opening, then select the saved battle and run count under **Strategy Simulation** and click **Simulate current rules** to view the report.
- **Chimera opening battle simulation:** Estimate whether the battle can meet its goals. If the forecast matches the live turns already played and predicts failure, the tool regroups within the configured limit. Set required trials or minimum damage under **Battle Goals**, enable **Simulate the whole battle at the opening**, and take over from the start.
- **Hydra opening devour-order forecast:** Check the expected mark order against your regroup conditions before the marks occur, and optionally check predicted final damage. Under **Battle Goals → Devour Order Regroup**, add a condition such as excluding a champion from the first N marks, enable **Forecast Devour Order at Battle Start (Experimental)**, and take over from the start.
- **Team setup preview and sharing:** Inspect the selected champions' stats, sets, blessings, relics, skills, and masteries, and include the setup when exporting a strategy. Select the team on a boss preparation screen and open **Current Team → Team setup**; after importing a strategy with a setup, open **Author's team**.
