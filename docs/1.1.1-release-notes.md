# RSL-Boss-helper 1.1.1

## 中文

更新后请**重启一次游戏**。策略、缓存和日志改存到 `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RSL-Boss-helper`，首次启动时会自动复制旧版本的数据。

- **六头蛇策略模拟：** 用保存的六头蛇开局，按正在编辑的规则离线重打，查看伤害、吞噬标记、阵亡、斩杀蛇头，以及开局推演是否会重整。工具从开局接管过一场六头蛇战斗后，在「策略模拟」选择这场开局，点击「模拟当前规则」。
- **模拟任意队伍：** 在「策略组队伍」卡片用「选择英雄」从自己的英雄中挑选队伍（游戏开着即可），或在准备界面点「设为准备界面的队伍」。然后在「策略模拟 → 队伍」选择「策略组当前队伍」「策略组保存时的队伍」，导入的策略还可以选「作者的队伍」。队伍不必打过这个 Boss。
- **更完整的模拟报告：** 一次最多模拟 100 场；「队伍配置」显示模拟用的队伍和属性；出手记录显示 Boss 用的技能（悬停看说明），以及每次出手引发的组队攻击、反击、激怒攻击和每名英雄的伤害，可以只看这类出手。
- **六头蛇目标：** 新增「防御最低蛇头」；「暴露蛇颈」改为优先选防御最低的。规则条件不满足时会依次检查下一个。
- **吞噬条件：** 「所选英雄从未被吞噬」改为真正被吞下才算，只被标记不再触发重整。
- **策略组按账号分开：** 每个游戏账号有自己的策略组，随所选游戏客户端登录的账号自动切换。
- 伤害统一使用游戏里的 K/M/B 写法；使用「当前伤害 ≥」条件的规则不再让六头蛇开局推演失效；修复队伍配置把非套装装备算漏、显示「装备 8/9」的问题。

## English

After updating, **restart the game once**. Strategies, caches and logs now live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RSL-Boss-helper`; the first start copies your data from the previous version.

- **Hydra strategy simulation:** Replay a saved Hydra opening offline with the rules you are editing and see damage, devour marks, deaths, heads killed, and whether the opening forecast would regroup. After the tool has played a Hydra battle from its opening, choose that battle under **Strategy Simulation** and click **Simulate current rules**.
- **Simulate any team:** On the **Strategy's team** card, pick the team with **Choose champions** (the game only needs to be open), or use **Use the preparation screen team** on the preparation screen. Then under **Strategy Simulation → Team** choose **Strategy's team now** or **Team when the strategy was saved**, or **The author's team** for an imported strategy. The team does not need to have fought that boss.
- **Richer simulation reports:** Up to 100 runs at once; **Team setup** shows the team and stats the simulation used; the **Action log** shows the bosses' skills (descriptions on hover) and, under each action, the ally attacks, counterattacks and provoked attacks it set off with each champion's damage, with a filter for these actions.
- **Hydra targets:** New **Lowest-DEF Head**; **Exposed Neck** now picks the lowest DEF first. When the rule's conditions do not hold for it, the next one is checked.
- **Devour condition:** **Selected Champions Are Never Devoured** now counts real swallows only; a mark alone no longer triggers a regroup.
- **Strategy groups per account:** Every game account keeps its own strategy groups, switched automatically by the account logged in to the chosen game client.
- Damage uses the game's K/M/B notation everywhere; rules with a "current damage ≥" condition no longer disable the Hydra opening forecast; fixed the team setup counting "equipped 8/9" when a champion wears a piece without a set.
